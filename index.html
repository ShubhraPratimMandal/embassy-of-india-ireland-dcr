Public Diplomatic Record

A chronological record of major Embassy developments reflected in the DCR RP process.

Embassy launched and recruitment opened2 October 2026 · Dublin City Roleplay
The Embassy's diplomatic Discord platform launched, with inaugural recruitment announced for Embassy Staff, Diplomatic Service and Consular Service.
Initial premises discussion: City Hall2–3 October 2026
City Hall was discussed as a possible diplomatic location. Following consultation with Irish authorities and DCR administration, the premises question was moved toward an alternative arrangement.
Diplomatic consultation with the Government of Ireland3 October 2026
The Ambassador and Irish officials met to address premises, security, access, shared use, safe-zone protections and future cooperation.
Alternative premises proposed: DMV3 October 2026
The Department of Motor Vehicle Transportation (DMV) building was proposed as a larger, more suitable Embassy location under an Irish-controlled licence.
Embassy Security framework discussed3 October 2026
The framework permits up to four armed Embassy Security members, requires appropriate licences, restricts movement outside the premises, and provides that weapons remain subject to the agreed RP restrictions.
Embassy safe-zone provision established3 October 2026
The negotiating parties established that no shooting would occur inside the Embassy premises, with emergency response and government consultation used for major incidents.
Good-faith assurance exchanged3 October 2026
The Embassy reaffirmed that it would avoid unnecessary confrontation, respect Irish law and the DCR framework, and cooperate with the host government.
Formal licence and agreement pending finalizationCurrent status
The negotiated terms are awaiting the final written licence/agreement to define the exact permitted activities, supervision, security arrangements and authorization procedures.
