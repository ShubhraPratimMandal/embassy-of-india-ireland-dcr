# Fictional Visa Framework

## Purpose
This framework governs the Embassy's fictional visa roleplay within Dublin City Roleplay.

## Categories
- Tourist / Visitor
- Business
- Student / Academic
- Cultural / Exchange
- Transit
- Diplomatic / Official
- Other approved RP category

## Application Flow
1. Applicant submits the Typeform application.
2. Consular staff review the RP information.
3. Additional clarification may be requested.
4. A fictional interview may be scheduled.
5. The RP application is marked approved or refused according to the scenario.
6. The applicant receives the case outcome through the designated Embassy channel.

## Privacy
Applicants should not submit:
- Passport numbers
- Government identification numbers
- Home addresses
- Financial information
- Passwords
- Unnecessary personal documents

Only roleplay information necessary for the scenario should be collected.

## Security
All visa decisions are fictional. No application creates immigration, visa, legal, governmental or travel status outside DCR.

## Interview
Applicants may be asked about purpose of travel, itinerary, host/accommodation, sponsorship and intended dates of the fictional visit.

## Disclaimer
The Embassy visa portal is a roleplay service only and does not represent a real immigration authority.
