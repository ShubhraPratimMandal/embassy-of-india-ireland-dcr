# Embassy Rules & Diplomatic Protocol

## 1. Institutional Status
The Embassy of the Republic of India to Ireland is a fictional diplomatic institution operating within Dublin City Roleplay (DCR) and ERLC.

It does not represent the real Government of India, the real Government of Ireland, or any real-world diplomatic mission.

## 2. Authority
All Embassy activities remain subject to:
- DCR Management
- DCR rules and regulations
- DCR moderation
- Applicable ERLC rules
- Approved RP procedures

DCR authority takes precedence over internal Embassy procedures.

## 3. Diplomatic Conduct
Embassy personnel must communicate professionally, respect representatives of other institutions, and avoid turning RP disagreements into personal disputes.

## 4. Diplomatic Premises
The Embassy's premises are treated as designated diplomatic RP premises under the applicable licence arrangement. The premises are not represented as Indian sovereign territory.

## 5. Access and Security
The negotiated premises framework provides for host-government authority and agreed access procedures. Embassy Security personnel must operate within the exact conditions approved by the host authority and DCR.

## 6. Safe-Zone Rule
The negotiated RP arrangement establishes the Embassy premises as a no-shooting safe zone. Armed incidents should be handled through the emergency procedures and authorities recognized by the RP framework.

## 7. Official Meetings
Official meetings should have a stated purpose, participating representatives, agenda, time, location and follow-up record where practical.

## 8. Correspondence
Official correspondence should be clear, professional, respectful, appropriately attributed and consistent with the sender's role.

## 9. Good-Faith Principle
The Embassy commits to resolving disagreements through consultation and to avoiding unnecessary confrontation.

## 10. Public Communications
Official statements should be issued only by authorized personnel. Public posts must distinguish fictional RP communications from real-world governmental statements.

## 11. Staff Conduct
Staff must not abuse permissions, fabricate approvals, misrepresent DCR Management, or use Embassy positions to claim authority outside approved RP.

## 12. Records
Significant diplomatic meetings, agreements, incidents and public statements may be recorded in the Embassy's public diplomatic record.
