# Public Diplomatic Record

## 2 October 2026 — Launch
The Embassy of the Republic of India to Ireland launched its diplomatic Discord server within Dublin City Roleplay and opened inaugural recruitment.

## 2–3 October 2026 — City Hall Discussion
City Hall was considered as a possible Embassy location. The parties subsequently explored alternative premises after concerns about continued Irish use of the building.

## 3 October 2026 — Bilateral Premises Meeting
The Ambassador and Irish officials discussed Embassy premises, licensing, shared use, diplomatic access, safe-zone rules, security and emergency coordination.

## 3 October 2026 — DMV Premises Proposal
The Department of Motor Vehicle Transportation building was selected as the preferred larger premises for the Embassy's RP functions, subject to an Irish-controlled licence.

## 3 October 2026 — Security Terms
A framework was discussed under which up to four armed Embassy Security members could operate, provided they held the required licence and followed the agreed movement and firearms restrictions.

## 3 October 2026 — Safe-Zone Provision
The negotiating parties established a no-shooting safe-zone rule for the Embassy premises.

## 3 October 2026 — Good-Faith Commitment
The Embassy reaffirmed its commitment to peaceful consultation, cooperation, respect for Irish law, and compliance with DCR rules.

## Current Status
A final written licence/agreement is pending. The final instrument is expected to define the permitted uses of the premises, supervision, access, security, emergency response, authorization requirements and termination procedures.

> This record summarizes fictional RP events and is not a statement of real-world diplomatic relations.
