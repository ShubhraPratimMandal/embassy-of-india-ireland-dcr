# Embassy Recruitment Guide

## Recruitment Tracks

### Embassy Staff
General administration, operations, event coordination, archives, public diplomacy support and general Embassy duties.

### Diplomatic Service
Government engagement, bilateral relations, diplomatic correspondence, official meetings, visits and protocol.

### Consular Service
Citizen assistance, appointments, fictional visa roleplay, public-facing services and consular administration.

## Assessment Principles
Applicants may be assessed on:
- Communication
- Professionalism
- Reliability
- Teamwork
- Roleplay quality
- Scenario judgment
- DCR / ERLC compliance
- Availability

## Process
1. Submit the appropriate application.
2. Embassy personnel review the application.
3. The applicant may be asked for additional information or an interview.
4. The responsible authority makes the appointment decision.
5. Successful applicants receive role and onboarding instructions.

## Appointments
Submitting an application does not guarantee appointment or rank.

Applicants should not publicly claim Embassy positions until formally appointed within the RP framework.

## Conduct
Recruitment staff must assess applicants consistently and without favoritism. Personal relationships should not determine appointment outcomes.

## DCR Oversight
All recruitment remains subject to DCR Management and DCR moderation.
